What this data measures — and what it doesn't
  • Assessed value is not market price or rent. California's Prop 13 caps assessed-value growth at ~2%/yr until a property changes hands, so medians here run far below market and move slowly. Use the shape of the trend (and jumps when properties sell), not the dollar level.
  • An eviction notice is not a completed eviction. These are notices filed with the SF Rent Board. Many are resolved, withdrawn, or never result in a tenant leaving. A notice can also cite more than one reason.
  • The rent benchmark is a separate, external estimate — not a listing. DataSF has no rent data, so asking-rent figures come from Zillow's rent index and Apartment List (both public). They show a neighborhood's typical rent and trend; per-bedroom figures are estimates, mapped by ZIP, and reflect all home types. Use them to calibrate expectations, not as a quote for any specific unit.

West Portal uses San Francisco’s broader “West of Twin Peaks” reporting area.
